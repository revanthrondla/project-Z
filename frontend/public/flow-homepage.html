Features

Everything your team needs
in one place

From candidate onboarding to final invoice — Flow handles the entire workflow so your team can focus on what matters.

Time Tracking

Smart Timesheets

Candidates and employees log hours in seconds. Managers approve with one click. Automatic overtime detection and weekly summaries included.

Absence Management

Absence & Leave Tracking

Manage holiday, sick leave, and other absences with configurable allowances and approval workflows. Integrated directly into timesheet calculations.

Invoicing

Automated Invoicing

Set billing rates per candidate or client. Flow auto-generates accurate invoices from approved timesheets — ready to send with a single click.

Multi-tenant

Multi-Company Support

Run multiple client organisations from one account. Each tenant has its own isolated data, branding, and user management — fully segregated.

Analytics

Reports & Analytics

Real-time dashboards for hours worked, costs, invoices raised, and absence trends. Export to PDF, Excel, or share directly with stakeholders.

Compliance

Audit Trail & Compliance

Every timesheet change, approval, and invoice is logged with a full audit trail. GDPR-compliant by design with data segregation per tenant.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

No lengthy onboarding. Flow is designed so you're invoicing clients before the end of your first day.

1

Set up your workspace

Create your organisation, add your team, and configure billing rates in under five minutes.

2

Team logs time

Candidates and employees submit timesheets weekly. Managers receive notifications and approve with one click.

3

Absences sync automatically

Approved leave is deducted from timesheets automatically, keeping hours and payroll accurate.

4

Invoice your clients

Flow generates itemised invoices from approved timesheets. Review, customise, and send — or let it run automatically.
