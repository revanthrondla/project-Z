How we work

Our working principles

1

Write before you speak

Ideas, decisions, and context are written down first. Meetings are for discussion, not information sharing.

2

Ship small, ship often

We prefer 10 small improvements to one big launch. Fast feedback loops keep quality high and momentum real.

3

Default to transparency

Salaries, company metrics, strategy — all shared with the team. No surprises, no politics.

4

Disagree and commit

We debate hard before decisions, but once made we move together. Revisit when you have new information.
