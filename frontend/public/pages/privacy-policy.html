Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 April 2025  ·  Effective: 1 April 2025

This policy explains how Flow ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use our platform. We are committed to handling your data lawfully, fairly, and transparently under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

Flow is operated by Flow Software Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (Company No. 14567890), with registered office at 42 Digital Quarter, London, EC1A 1BB. Flow Software Ltd is the data controller for personal data processed through the Flow platform.

Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at dpo@flowapp.io.

2. What data we collect

Account and identity data

When you or your organisation creates a Flow account, we collect: name, email address, job title, company name, and a password (stored as a salted hash — never in plain text).

Usage data

We automatically collect information about how you use Flow, including: pages visited, features used, timestamps, IP address, browser type and version, and device information. This data is used to improve the product and detect misuse.

Workforce data (as data processor)

When your organisation uses Flow to manage timesheets, absences, and invoices, we process personal data about your employees and candidates on your behalf. In this context, your organisation is the data controller and we act as the data processor. This data may include: names, contact details, hours worked, leave records, and payment information. We handle this data strictly in accordance with your instructions and our Data Processing Agreement.

Communication data

If you contact us by email or through our support portal, we retain records of those communications to resolve your enquiry and improve our service.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Flow platform
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • To send you service-related notifications (e.g. invoice alerts, timesheet reminders)
  • To send marketing communications where you have opted in (you may opt out at any time)
  • To analyse usage patterns and improve product features
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Legal basis for processing

We process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract: processing necessary to fulfil your subscription agreement with us
  • Legitimate interests: improving our product, preventing fraud, and ensuring security
  • Consent: marketing emails and optional analytics cookies
  • Legal obligation: compliance with applicable law

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Sub-processors who help us deliver the service (e.g. cloud hosting, email delivery, payment processing). All sub-processors are bound by data processing agreements and are located in the UK or EEA, or subject to appropriate safeguards.
  • Professional advisers (lawyers, auditors) under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Law enforcement where we are legally required to do so.

A current list of our sub-processors is available on request by emailing dpo@flowapp.io.

6. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your subscription is active and for up to 7 years thereafter to comply with legal and financial obligations. Workforce data (timesheets, absences, invoices) is retained for 7 years from creation to satisfy HMRC requirements. You may request earlier deletion subject to legal constraints — see Your Rights below.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data where there is no legitimate reason to retain it
  • Restriction — ask us to restrict processing of your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time

To exercise any right, email dpo@flowapp.io. We will respond within 30 days. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

8. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies. Please see our Cookie Policy for full details. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner.

9. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including: encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3), multi-factor authentication options, strict access controls, regular penetration testing, and an incident response process aligned with ICO reporting requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email and/or by displaying a notice in the Flow application at least 14 days before changes take effect. Continued use of Flow after that date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact us

For questions about this policy or how we handle your data:

  • Email: dpo@flowapp.io
  • Post: Data Protection Officer, Flow Software Ltd, 42 Digital Quarter, London, EC1A 1BB
