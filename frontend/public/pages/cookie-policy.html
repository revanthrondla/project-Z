Last updated: 1 April 2025

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site owners. Cookies can be session-based (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent (stored on your device for a set period).

2. Cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the Flow application to function. They cannot be disabled. They include:

  • flow_session — authenticates your login session. Expires on browser close (session cookie).
  • flow_csrf — protects against cross-site request forgery attacks. Session cookie.
  • flow_tenant — identifies which tenant workspace you belong to. Persistent, 30 days.

Analytics cookies (optional)

We use these to understand how people use Flow so we can improve it. These are only set with your consent.

  • _flow_analytics — tracks page views and feature usage. Persistent, 1 year. All data is anonymised before processing.

Preference cookies (optional)

These remember your display preferences (e.g. dark mode, table density).

  • flow_prefs — stores UI preferences. Persistent, 1 year.

3. Third-party cookies

Our marketing website (but not the application) may include cookies from the following third parties when you consent to analytics:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — usage analytics. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We use IP anonymisation and have disabled data sharing with Google.

Within the Flow application, no third-party advertising or tracking cookies are used.

4. Managing your preferences

When you first visit Flow, you'll see a cookie banner where you can accept or decline optional cookies. You can change your preferences at any time:

  • Cookie banner: Click "Manage cookies" in the footer of our marketing site to update your preferences.
  • Browser settings: Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from logging in.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our use of cookies changes. We will display a notice on our website when we make material changes.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies: privacy@flowapp.io
