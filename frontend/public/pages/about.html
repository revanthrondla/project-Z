We're building the workforce
platform teams deserve

Flow was founded by people who spent years frustrated by clunky spreadsheets, disconnected payroll tools, and error-prone invoicing. We decided to fix it.

Our mission is to give every staffing agency and growing team a single, beautiful workspace where time, absence, and money flow together seamlessly.
Our Story

Built from frustration.
Refined with love.

In 2022, our founders were running a mid-size staffing agency and spending every Monday morning manually reconciling timesheets in Excel before generating invoices in a separate tool — then chasing approvals via email. It was costing the business hours every week and errors were costing money.

We looked at what was available: expensive enterprise platforms that needed six months to implement, or basic tools that didn't talk to each other. Neither was right. So we built Flow — a single, opinionated platform that does exactly what staffing teams need, nothing they don't.

Our Values

What drives everything we build

🎯

Relentless simplicity

Every feature we ship has to be simpler than the problem it replaces. If it adds cognitive load, we rethink it.

🤝

Customer obsession

We talk to customers every week. Their frustrations drive our roadmap, not our assumptions about what they need.

🔒

Trust through transparency

Clear pricing, honest timelines, GDPR-compliant by design. We earn trust by being straightforward about everything.

⚡

Move with purpose

🌱

Long-term thinking

We build for the next 10 years. That means strong data foundations, clean architecture, and no shortcuts that hurt users.

🌍

Inclusive by default
